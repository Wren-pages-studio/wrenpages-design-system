# Brand

**Positioning.** For small businesses that need a website that actually brings customers, Wren Pages builds fast,
friendly landing pages and small sites at a small-business price, with no agency jargon and no surprises.

**Tagline.** Small sites. Big first impressions.

## Tone of voice

- **Warm** — a neighbor who happens to know the web.
- **Direct** — short sentences, plain English.
- **No jargon** — no “synergies”, no “solutions”.
- **“We” voice** — the studio speaks as _we_; signed “— The Wren Pages team”.
- **Promises in numbers** — “loads in under 2s”, “~7 days”, “2 rounds of edits”.

| ✓ Do                                | ✕ Don’t                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| “We build fast, friendly websites.” | “Our team of experts leverages synergies.” |

**No founder, no faces.** Never use the founder’s name, photo, face or signature, and no corporate stock photos of
people. Visuals = the bird, phone/site mockups, screens.

# Logo

A wren (small, chubby, short tail cocked up) whose wing is a page with a folded corner. Honey bird in a Forest
circle, Forest eye; flat, max 3 colors. Wordmark: Fraunces SemiBold 600, outlined to paths.
